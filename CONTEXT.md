# Evictor

Evictor is an observability and control product for teams running AI inference
on serverless GPU providers.

## Language

**Validation Gate**:
The evidence threshold Evictor must pass before general product development
continues.
_Avoid_: Research phase, discovery checkbox

**Commitment**:
Observable effort from a target user, such as sharing sample data, trying a
prototype, or scheduling an installation.
_Avoid_: Interest, positive feedback, encouragement

**Validation Work**:
Work whose direct purpose is earning or testing target-user commitments.
_Avoid_: General development, roadmap progress
